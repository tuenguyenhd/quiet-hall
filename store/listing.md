## Long description

gm. Quiet Hall is a tiny shared meditation world for Solana people.

Walk into the hall, pick a cushion and sit. Everyone you see is a real person sitting with you right now, from anywhere in the world. No chat, no feed, no charts: just breathe together for 3, 5, 10 or 20 minutes.

• Pick your fren: a random Degen PFP character (laser eyes, samurai, ninjas, anime chibis, robots…) or a Monk, Shibu, Froggo, Kitty or Capy
• Adopt a pet: a Shiba, Husky or Frog follows you everywhere and sits beside you
• Get blessed: tap the resident frens (Shibu, Froggo, Old Monk…) to pat them, or get their blessing sparkles
• Light a lantern with a wish and watch it float over the lake for everyone
• Own a stone in the Stone Garden or a tree in the Maple Grove, a real NFT with your name on it
• Group bell every hour and a daily World Sit, so you always have company
• Earn badges by sitting, never by paying
• Seeker owners get a gold light, their own Seeker hall, and longer free lanterns and blessings

Pay with SOL, USDC or SKR through your wallet (Mobile Wallet Adapter / Seed Vault). Everything paid is cosmetic with a fixed price: no loot boxes, no odds, no promises of value.

Works offline for solo sits. Light on your phone.
