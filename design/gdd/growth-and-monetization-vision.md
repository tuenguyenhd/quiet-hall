# Growth and Monetization Vision (pitch material)

> **Status**: Vision draft, written at the owner's request. Not part of review pass 1's document set; the creative-director reviewer was told of its aim and advised against pitching the 🔴 items (see §9, point 6). Nothing here is a build commitment or a forecast.
> **Author**: owner + Claude
> **Last Updated**: 2026-11-10
> **Purpose**: show how far the game can go after the hype phase, for pitches (hackathons, Colosseum, grants, investors). Every item is tagged with what it needs before it can ship.
> **Reads with**: `master-gdd.md` (what is planned), `edition-seeker.md`, `edition-near.md`.

## 1. The pitch in four lines

1. **What we ship first is the wedge:** a free, calm, funny world that spreads by itself (shared sits, lost frens with serial numbers, an AI golem the whole server tries to talk open).
2. **What that gives us is rare:** a daily habit, a crowd of verified humans (one Seeker = one person), AI characters people already talk to, and an item economy with working rails.
3. **What we add next is depth:** more games inside the world, including finance-flavoured and competitive ones, each reusing the same crowd, currency, items and AI.
4. **What that becomes is a business:** cosmetics and passes first, then partner drops, premium AI, and (where the law and the stores allow) a real-value item market.

## 2. Two lanes (the rule that makes the rest possible)

| | **Leaves lane** | **Value lane** |
|---|---|---|
| Currency | Leaves (earned by sitting; no cash value) | SOL, USDC, SKR, card |
| What it buys | Valley and market play | Paid cosmetics today; the items in §4–§6 later |
| On-chain | Receipts only | Yes |
| Who can use it | Everyone, free | Opt-in; some features by region, age or verification |
| Store position | A game with play money | Standard purchases; anything finance-like is gated |

**The two lanes never convert into each other.** Anything earned or bought with Leaves stays play-only for good: it can never be exported or sold for money (`master-gdd.md` Pillar 4). Anything with real value is bought with real money in the value lane from the start. That keeps the free game safe for stores and for calm players, and lets the value lane grow as far as rules allow without putting the core at risk.

## 3. Readiness tags used below

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 🟢 **Ready** | Fits today's pillars and store rules. Needs only design and build. |
| 🟡 **Design care** | Possible, but needs a rule change in the constitution, careful wording, or store pre-review. |
| 🔴 **Legal first** | Involves real-money risk, prizes, tokens or financial activity. Needs legal advice, likely licences, region and age gating. Not started without that. |

## 7. Revenue ladder (what turns on when)

| Phase | Goal | Turns on | Money |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Hype** (now → launch) | People try it and share it | Free game, share cards, Vault wall, serials | None needed; tips |
| **2. Habit** | They come back | Leaves, valley, market, bonds | Fixed-price cosmetics (exists), season pass |
| **3. Community** | They bring others | Cabals, Dens, partner drops, festivals | Sponsorships, numbered editions, premium AI seat |
| **4. Arena** | They compete | Paper Degen, Agent Arena, leagues, duels | Subscriptions, sponsor prize events (where legal) |
| **5. Value** | They own and trade | Real-value item market, creator royalties, agent wallets | Marketplace fees, royalties, partner fee share |

Each phase starts only when the one before it is measurably working. Suggested gates (to be set with real data, not promises): Phase 2 when day-7 retention holds; Phase 3 when a meaningful share of players have a friend in the game; Phase 4 and 5 only after legal review and a store pre-check.

## 8. What each step asks of the constitution

| Step | Change needed |
|---|---|
| Season pass, numbered editions, premium AI | None (fixed price, no random rewards) |
| Partner drops and sponsored characters | Add: "Partners may sponsor content. No promotion of token prices or returns." |
| Duels and finance-flavoured games in the store build | Decide per store; keep behind flags; label "points only" |
| Real-value tradable items | Amend "Play money only" to the two-lane rule in §2; region gating |
| Prizes, staking, real trades | New legal framework; separate product surface if the store requires it |

## 9. Honest risks to state in a pitch

1. **Token-first and money-first games usually die.** A report cited in our own research (`../../../research/degen-games/2026-11-09.md`) says most Web3 games did, for that reason. Our order is the opposite: game and habit first, value last.
2. **Gambling and financial rules** decide what Phase 4–5 can be, country by country. We present those items as options under review, not as plans with dates.
3. **Store policy**: the calm free game must never depend on a 🔴 feature, so a rejection there cannot take the product down.
4. **Tone**: calm players must never be pushed toward the arena. Zoning (Pillar 1) is what lets one world hold both moods.
5. **One developer**: the ladder assumes funding or collaborators from Phase 3 on.
6. **Internal dissent, recorded:** the creative-director review of the GDD set (`reviews/review-pass-1-2026-11-10.md`) held that cash-value items, staked play, finance or trading bots that act for the player, and any monetization reaching into a sit should be anti-pillars rather than roadmap items, and that pitching them raises the store-review risk. The owner asked for them to be shown as potential. They stay here tagged 🔴, as options under review, never as plans with dates.
