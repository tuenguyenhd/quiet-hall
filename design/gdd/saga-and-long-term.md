## Summary

The long game: a five-chapter story that the community advances by sitting together, and the systems that give a player something to care about for months (bonds, expeditions, a home, calm trades, guilds, mentoring). Everything here only ever adds. Nothing is lost by staying away.

## Overview

Chapter 0 is the valley MVP. Each later chapter opens a place, a system and a piece of the mystery of the Great Rug. Chapters open when the community's shared sit minutes reach a goal; the goal can only be approached, never pushed back. Around the story sit long-term systems in four layers: **attachment** (bonds with rescued frens), **routine** (expeditions, fishing, gardens), **place** (your Den, the rebuilt village) and **people** (Cabals, mentors). Most content comes from clocks, the community and AI rather than hand-written quests.

**The governing rule:** the world can thank you for sitting; it can never punish you for not sitting. No storms, no quotas, no wilting, no decay.

## Player Fantasy

"This valley is a bit better because I sat here." Over months: my frens know me, my Den looks like me, our Cabal's garden is full of lanterns, and I was there the night the moon came up. And when I was away for a month, everything was exactly as I left it.

## Detailed Design

### Core Rules: story

| Ch. | Title | Opens when | Story beat | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Lost Frens | Valley launch | Frens are scattered. Shibby holds a torn map. | Valley MVP |
| 1 | The Torn Map | 2 weeks after launch | Each first rescue gives a map piece (5). Elder Hodl tells of Moon Village. | Bonds, expeditions, variants, 5 more frens |
| 2 | Into the FUD | Fog goal 1 | The fog thins; deep caves open. Paperhands the raccoon sells your clues. | Fishing, deep nodes, village donations |
| 3 | Rebuild Moon Village | Fog goal 2 | Shared building projects. The whale surfaces in Wen Moon Lake when 30 or more players sit on its shore. | Dens, gardens, Cabals |
| 4 | Who Pulled the Rug? | All 6 village projects done **and** 700 Vault relics found server-wide | Nobody pulled it: everyone ran at once. Kettle is the old Dev who stayed. The village rings the mended Bell at a World Sit and the moon rises. | Moon Festival, lands beyond, seasons |

1. Scenes are short (≤ 6 lines), told through Elder Hodl, wish cards, Rescue Book scrolls and Vault notes. Nothing longer than 20 s; all skippable.
2. Late joiners get a recap scroll and play in the current world.
3. The story's moral is "the Rug was everyone panicking at once; calm is what holds". The game must not contradict it with systems that reward panic.

### Core Rules: the fog (Month 2)

4. Fog covers the deep parts of the valley. Inside it, areas are closed.
5. The fog **only retreats**. Each time the community's shared sit minutes reach a goal (Formulas), it retreats one step, for good.
6. Any sit anywhere counts, including in the Quiet Hall by players who never enter the valley. The Lookout scroll tells them what their sitting did.
7. There are no storms and no setbacks.

### Core Rules: attachment (Month 1)

**Bonds**
8. Each rescued fren has **hearts** and a bond level 1–10. Hearts never decrease.
9. Hearts come from: rescuing that fren again (+3); sitting with it resting beside you, anywhere including the hall (+1 per 5 full minutes, max 6 a day across all frens); giving it a drip it has not worn before (+5); collecting its expedition (+2).
10. Levels unlock, in order: story scroll part 1, an emote, a second idle pose, scroll part 2, a pointing hint (it looks toward the area holding a material your open wish needs), a name tag colour, scroll part 3, a sleeping pose in your Den, a duet emote with another fren, its "true name" title. No level gives stats, speed or yield.

**Expeditions**
11. One fren that is not following you can be sent on a 2, 4 or 8 hour trip. One slot.
12. It returns with a fixed, shown-in-advance bundle of its home material (`meme-valley.md` wish table) and sometimes a postcard (a seeded lore line). No random rares.
13. Collecting is manual, at most 2 collections per UTC day. Snack prices (`leaves-economy.md`) are set so a trip never returns more Leaves' worth of materials than it cost.
14. An uncollected trip waits forever. Nothing spoils.

### Core Rules: routine (Month 2+)

15. **Fishing** (Wen Moon Lake): cast, wait for a ripple, tap within 1.5 s. 12 fish with place and time-of-day rules, recorded as a section of the Rescue Book (one book, more pages).
16. **Village donations** (from chapter 2): give materials or Leaves to the current project. Every contributor who gives at least 50 Leaves of value gets that project's bound relic. No ranking of contributors.
17. **Gardens** (chapter 3): 4 plots at your Den. A seed grows in 8–24 real hours. Plants never wilt or die. Sitting 3 or more minutes adds a bonus flower to each ripe plant you harvest that day; not sitting just means no bonus.
18. **Tea**: fish + herbs → a 1-hour cosmetic steam aura. Never overrides a paid blessing.
19. **Valley craft level** (Month 4+): one level 1–20 from gathering, crafting, fishing and gardening together. Unlocks recipes, dyes and titles only.

### Core Rules: place (Month 4+)

20. **Den**: a private instanced room off the Gate. Place decor on a grid; your frens idle there; up to 8 visitors; each visitor can leave one "gm" leaf a day on your door.
21. **Village projects**: 6 shared builds (shrine, bridge, bell tower, tea stall, bath, moon gate), one at a time, each a permanent change to the world.

### Core Rules: people (Month 4+)

22. **Cabals** (guilds): up to 30 members, a name, a banner, a shared garden of 12 plots and a lantern rack. Each member's sit minutes light lanterns on the rack (Formulas); lanterns only accumulate. There is no quota, no weekly target and no ranking between Cabals. The treasury takes Leaves gifts for Cabal decor only; nothing can be withdrawn.
23. **Mentors**: a Trusted player with 30 or more sit days can adopt up to 3 New players. When a mentee becomes Trusted and has 3 first rescues, the mentor gets a title step and a mentor-only drip; the mentee gets a starter drip.
24. **Stalls** (Trading Hall, after the trade gate): rent a stall for 24 hours, list up to 6 items at fixed prices within the trade limits; sales happen while you are away.

### Core Rules: Cabals in depth (lessons from MMO guilds)

25. **Cabal Hall:** a shared instanced Den all members decorate; every member's rescued frens wander there.
26. **Ranks:** Founder, Elders (up to 3), Members. Elders can invite and decorate; only the Founder can rename or dissolve.
27. **Recruitment board** at the Gate: each Cabal posts one line ("quiet morning sitters, EU"); players request to join.
28. **Caravan stall:** when 3 or more members sit side by side on Bazaar mats, they open one shared stall of 6 items (after the trade gate).
29. **Team Cup bracket** (Trading Hall only): a Cabal's score is the **average** of its 3 best entrants, so small Cabals compete fairly. Play chips only.
30. **No duty, no decay:** no attendance rules, no quotas, no automatic kicks. An inactive member keeps their place and their lanterns. Two Cabals may merge by vote of both Founders.
31. **Crest:** a bound NEAR receipt for founding members (NEAR edition), a cosmetic crest on collars everywhere.

What MMO guilds get wrong and this avoids: attendance and raid duty (burnout), big guilds crushing small ones (averaged scores), dead guilds (merge, "last sat together" shown).

### Core Rules: events (lessons from MMO world events)

| Event | Rhythm | How it works | Mood |
|---|---|---|---|
| **World Sit** | Daily (shipped) | Everyone sits together at 20:00 UTC | Calm |
| **Calm the FUD Golem** | Weekly, rotating hour | A fog creature drifts into the valley. Its panic bar falls 1 point per minute any player sits within 15 m, for 30 minutes. Calmed → everyone who sat there gets a bound trophy and a scroll. Not calmed → it drifts off and returns next week. Nothing is lost. | Calm, together (a raid whose weapon is meditation) |
| **Lost Herd** | Surprise, about twice a week | 10 frens appear together for 1 hour; co-rescues in that hour count double toward bond hearts | Together |
| **Whale Order** | Weekly | Kettle posts one giant order (e.g. 2,000 Moon Rocks in 48 hours). Contributions from anyone; Cabals see a team total. If filled, every contributor gets a share of a fixed Leaves pool by contribution, and a bound trophy. | Together |
| **Rug Night / Bull Hour** | Monthly | For one hour Kettle's market goes wild: scripted swings in one item's price (Rug Night) or a demand spike (Bull Hour). Play money, Trading Hall only. | Lively |
| **Moon Festival** | Monthly, real full moon | Festival craft, Full Moon Mewn, lanterns over the lake | Calm |

Event rules: at most one big event a week; start hours rotate across time zones; event cosmetics return in later years (no permanent loss); no new event currency (rewards are cosmetics, trophies and shares of a fixed Leaves pool inside the economy doc's budget).

### States and Transitions

**Chapter:** `locked → goal in progress (public bar, only fills) → open → recap`.

**Fog step:** `closed → open` (one way).

**Bond:** level n → n + 1 at the heart threshold; never down.

**Expedition:** `idle → away (2 / 4 / 8 h) → back (waits indefinitely) → collected → idle`. An away fren cannot follow, be dressed or be sent again.

**Plot:** `empty → planted → growing → ripe (waits indefinitely) → harvested → empty`.

**Village project:** `open (bar fills) → built` (one way).

## Formulas

**Fog goal** (shared sit minutes for step `s`): `goal(s) = GOAL_BASE × DAU7 × GOAL_DAYS(s)`, `GOAL_BASE` = 12 minutes per daily player per day, `DAU7` fixed when the goal is set (minimum 50), `GOAL_DAYS` = 14, 21. Example: 200 daily players, step 1 → 12 × 200 × 14 = 33,600 minutes. If the bar is under 50% after twice the planned days, the goal is lowered to current progress + 20% ("the fog thins").

**Bond thresholds** (cumulative hearts for level L): `hearts(L) = round( 6 × (L − 1)^1.6 )` → 6, 18, 35, 55, 79, 105, 135, 167, 202 for L2–L10. At about 8 hearts a day on one fren, level 10 takes about 25 days.

**Expedition bundle** (units of home material): 2 h → 4, 4 h → 7, 8 h → 12. Value at floor price for a 2-Leaf material: 8 / 14 / 24; snacks 10 / 18 / 30, so every trip costs more Leaves than its materials would sell for. Expeditions are a convenience and a sink, never a faucet.

**Cabal lanterns**: `lanterns = floor( total member sit minutes since founding / 100 )`. 30 members sitting 10 minutes a day light 3 a day.

**Server-wide relics for chapter 4**: 700. At about 15 cracks a day (`vault-whisperer-npc.md`) this takes roughly 7 weeks after the Vault opens.

## Edge Cases

| Case | Handling |
|---|---|
| Player count collapses mid-goal | "The fog thins" rule lowers the goal. |
| Player count explodes | The goal completes early. Accepted. |
| A player only ever sits | They advance fog goals and get the chapter marks; the Lookout scroll credits them. |
| A fren is away when its wish comes round | The rescue works and gives hearts. |
| Player gone for 3 months | Balance, bonds, garden, Den, Cabal membership and expedition are exactly as left. |
| Cabal leader leaves | The longest-standing member becomes leader. |
| Mentor farming fake mentees | Mentees must reach Trusted and 3 first rescues; flag rules apply; 3 at a time, 12 a season. |
| Tea while a paid blessing runs | Refused with a note. |
| Bond hearts from sitting abused by idling | Same sit rule and the daily cap of 6. |

## Visual/Audio Requirements

Fog as a soft wall with a visible edge that rolls back; the whale as one large slow model; the moonrise as a one-time sky event with a replay scroll. Story scenes use portraits and text, no voice.

## Game Feel

Seasons, not sprints. A bar the whole server nudges forward, a fren that greets you a little differently this week, a rack of lanterns that was shorter last month.

## UI Requirements

- Chapter bar on the Gate signpost and the Lookout scroll (never in the hall).
- Fren sheet: hearts, level, next unlock, Send on expedition.
- Den edit mode: grid, drag, rotate; one-thumb.
- Cabal sheet: members, lantern rack, garden.
- Calendar scroll: next full moon, festival.

## Acceptance Criteria

For the first slice to be built (bonds, expeditions). Later systems get criteria in their own GDDs.

1. Given a fren with 17 hearts, then it is level 2; at 18 it is level 3 and the level-3 unlock is usable.
2. Given 30 counted sit minutes with a fren beside the player, then 6 hearts are added; a further 30 minutes the same day add 0.
3. Given the bond unlock table, then no entry changes pick time, yield, movement speed, a price or a cap (static check of the data file).
4. Given a 4-hour trip started at T with the 18-Leaf snack, then 18 Leaves are spent at T, collection before T + 4 h is refused, and collection after gives exactly 7 units and 2 hearts.
5. Given 2 collections today, then a third is refused.
6. Given a fren on a trip, then it is not shown following and cannot be equipped.
7. Given any expedition length and home material, then `bundle × floor price < snack price` (unit test on the data file).
8. Given an account with no activity for 90 simulated days, then its balance, hearts, bag and pending expedition are unchanged.
9. Given the saga data and routes, then no rule reduces a stored value because of elapsed time or missing sit minutes (static check: no negative delta with a time-based reason).
