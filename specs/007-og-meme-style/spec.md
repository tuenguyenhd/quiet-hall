# Spec 007: Solana OG meme style pass (after 005 is tested)

## Why
The Seeker audience knows Solana culture. A light touch of that humour makes share cards and avatars
feel "ours" and gets reposted, while the hall stays calm.

## Direction
- **Palette accents:** Solana-style purple → green gradient (#9945FF → #14F195) used sparingly: avatar lights,
  item glows, share card frame, Seeker gold stays gold.
- **No avatar costumes** (owner decision 2026-11-09). The meme touch goes on badges (icon art), lanterns
  (colours, preset wishes like "gm", "wagmi"), stone/tree plaques, share cards and small world details.
- **Copy tone:** "gm" on morning entry, "wagmi: we're all gonna meditate", share line
  "sat 10 min. touched grass." Kept short and optional.
- **Not allowed:** real meme coin mascots or logos (dogs in hats from specific projects, etc.), Solana logo
  as a game object, anything that looks like an official Solana product.

## Sources
- Procedural/own art first. CC0 packs (Kenney, Quaternius, Poly Pizza CC0) for hats/props if they match;
  every asset recorded in `public/assets/CREDITS.md` with link + licence.

## Acceptance
- Screenshot review: avatar + cosmetics + world feel like one style (soft clay + playful accents).
- Share card shows the cosmetic and the gradient accent.

## Update 2026-11-09: characters + sticker UI
- Owner asked for meme-type characters. Free pick on the welcome card (no sale): Monk, Shibu (pixel shades, curly tail), Froggo, Kitty (gold chain), Capy (orange on head). Original low-poly designs, no copied meme art. Network: colour index = species × 8 + light.
- Seven resident frens sit around the world and say a line when tapped or when you walk close.
- UI: "sticker" look (cream panels, thick ink outline, hard offset shadow, Lilita One display font, Solana purple/green + yellow/pink); dusk sky.
- World: hall 22×16 m with 24 cushions, two-tier roof, neon strip + "gm" plaque; places ~1.45× further apart; bounds 82 m (server clamp now uses the same value).
